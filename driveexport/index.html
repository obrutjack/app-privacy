Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Thank you for using DriveExport for Mac ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect or store any personally identifiable information on our servers. All data (exported files, preferences, settings) is stored locally on your device only.

2. Google Account Authorization

The App uses OAuth 2.0 to access your Google Drive. Specifically:

  • Permission scope: drive.readonly — read-only access only. The App never modifies, deletes, or uploads any files on your Google Drive.
  • Token storage: Access credentials (Access Token and Refresh Token) are encrypted and stored in your device's macOS Keychain, and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Data flow: Files from Google Drive are downloaded directly to your Mac — they never pass through any third-party server.
  • Revoking access: You can sign out at any time from the App's preferences, or visit Google Account Permissions to revoke authorization.

Your Google Account data usage is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Exported Files

Files exported from Google Drive (in .docx, .xlsx, .pptx, .pdf, and other formats) are saved only to the local Mac folder you specify. They are never uploaded to any external service.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, etc.). The only external connection is direct communication with the Google Drive API (googleapis.com) to read the files you have authorized.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Data Security

The App does not transmit any personal data to our servers. Your Google Account credentials are encrypted by macOS Keychain; exported files are protected by macOS file system access controls.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

本應用程式不收集、不儲存任何個人識別資料至我們的伺服器。所有資料均儲存於您的裝置本機。

本應用程式不使用任何第三方分析、廣告或追蹤服務。
